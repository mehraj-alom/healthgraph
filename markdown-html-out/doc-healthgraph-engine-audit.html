HealthGraph Engine Audit: What Each Engine Must Add to Replace Real Apps

!IMPORTANT
Biggest single gap: no Apple HealthKit / Google Health Connect / wearable sync. Every competing engine assumes passive data collection, while HealthGraph is still mostly manual logging plus camera spot checks.
iNOTE
Wellness guardrail preserved: this audit does not recommend diagnosis, blood-pressure estimation, AFib detection, or treatment advice. Those would change the app's regulatory posture.

How to read this document

Each engine section names the real-world apps it must replace, lists what exists today, then lists what is missing and what to add. A priority build order follows at the end.

1. HeartEngine + PPG measurement

Must replace: Welltory, HRV4Training, Optimal HRV, Apple Watch Vitals/Heart, Fitbit resting-HR/HRV tracking.

Have today: fingertip PPG plus face RPPG spot checks; BPM, signal quality, and reliability gating; RMSSD/SDNN for fingertip measurements; 7-day average and recent history; derived-vitals-only backend with no video uploaded.

Missing: no wearable or HealthKit/Health Connect import; no resting vs active context; no HR zones; no SpO2 or respiratory-rate feature set; HRV unavailable for face measurements; no measurement reminders; no calibration or accuracy story; no per-engine export.

Add: HealthKit/Health Connect heart-rate import with source labels; resting-HR tagging and morning-reading workflow; HR zones; extended HRV coverage or clear face-RPPG limits; 30/90-day trends with min/max/range; measurement reminders; device accuracy disclosure; per-engine CSV/PDF export. Do not add BP estimation, ECG diagnosis, or AFib claims.

2. SleepEngine

Must replace: Sleep Cycle, Pillow, Rise, Oura sleep, Whoop sleep, Fitbit sleep.

Have today: manual sleep duration, 1-5 quality, awakenings; 28-day average, 7-day strip, 14-night history.

Missing: no bedtime/wake timestamps; no consistency or sleep-debt score; no sleep stages; no snore/audio detection; no smart alarm; no wearable sleep import; no sleep score, goals, reminders, coaching, or nap handling.

Add: bed/wake timestamps and sleep schedule; consistency score and sleep debt; wearable and HealthKit sleep-stage import; optional snore detection; smart alarm; bedtime reminders and sleep goal; sleep coaching tied to recovery; nap logging; sleep history export.

3. NutritionEngine + FoodScan

Must replace: MyFitnessPal, Cronometer, Lose It, Yazio, MacroFactor.

Have today: meal-photo AI estimate; barcode lookup; calories plus protein/carbs/fat; editable macro targets and daily calorie ring; meal history.

Missing: fallback scans can be kcal-only with zeroed macros; no fiber, sugar, sodium, or micronutrients; no diet templates or allergy flags; no recipe builder, favorites, recents, or templates; no voice logging; no water reminders; weak barcode-miss workflow; less database depth than leaders.

Add: fiber/sugar/sodium plus core micronutrients; diet modes such as high-protein, low-carb, and Mediterranean-style; allergen and intolerance flags; portion-correction UI after AI estimation; recipe builder, favorites, recents, meal duplication; voice logging; water reminders; barcode-miss submission workflow; verified-food curation; nutrition export.

4. FitnessEngine

Must replace: Strong, Hevy, Fitbod, Strava, Apple Fitness.

Have today: manual activity type, duration, calories; session counts, active minutes, deltas, history.

Missing: no structured sets/reps/weight; no workout templates or plans; no rest timer; no GPS route/distance; no steps/distance; no HR zones; no personal records; no wearable workout import; no progression or recovery-aware programming.

Add: structured strength logging with exercise, sets, reps, weight, RPE; workout templates and reusable routines; rest timer; personal records and volume progression; optional GPS tracking; steps/distance via platform sync; workout reminders and weekly goal; workout export.

5. WeightEngine

Must replace: Happy Scale, Bodly, Withings/Renpho scale apps, FatSecret weight tracking.

Have today: profile weight display; graph only when DailyLog weight history exists.

Missing: no goal or goal range; no BMI/body-fat/measurements; no trend smoothing; no smart-scale sync; no weigh-in reminders; no contextual notes for spikes.

Add: goal weight or healthy range; BMI, body fat, waist/hip measurements; trend smoothing and milestone view; smart-scale import via HealthKit/Health Connect; weigh-in reminders; spike-context notes; progress-photo linkage; export.

6. WellbeingEngine

Must replace: Daylio, Bearable, MindDoc, Apple State of Mind.

Have today: 1-5 mood, energy, stress; averages, deltas, 14-day history.

Missing: no emotion tags or contributing factors; no journaling linkage; no symptom/medication/sleep correlation depth; no goals; no check-in reminders outside voice; no weekly reflection or AI brief; no export except global report paths.

Add: emotion tags and activity/factor tagging; journal linkage; check-in reminders; weekly reflection and pattern brief; cross-factor correlations with actions; goals tied to sleep, activity, and stress; shareable wellness summary; CSV/PDF export. Only add PHQ/GAD-style instruments if the team accepts medical-device scope.

7. Recovery / Readiness

Must replace: Whoop Recovery, Oura Readiness, Garmin Training Readiness/Body Battery.

Have today: 0-100 score with Ready/Steady/Take-it-easy bands; factor bars and sparse-data guidance.

Missing: no overnight wearable physiology; HRV often unavailable; no sleep-stage input; no strain/load target; no training recommendation; no lifestyle-habit correlation; no transparent baseline explanation; no calibration-period handling.

Add: overnight HR/HRV/sleep import from wearables; personal baseline display and calibration period; daily strain/load target; actionable guidance such as train, easy activity, or rest; habit correlation for alcohol, caffeine, late meals, and sleep timing; recovery trends; export.

8. Cycle

Must replace: Flo, Clue, Apple Cycle Tracking.

Have today: calendar, period spans, notes; add/edit/delete; device-only storage.

Missing: no period prediction; no fertile-window/ovulation prediction; no pregnancy mode; no symptom linkage; no reminders; no partner sharing; no doctor-report integration; no wearable temperature/HR signals; no anonymous/privacy mode story.

Add: period predictions; fertile-window and ovulation estimates; symptom linkage across mood, pain, sleep, and energy; TTC/pregnancy/perimenopause modes or an explicitly narrower scope; period and symptom reminders; cycle-aware insights; doctor-report export; strong privacy controls and optional anonymous mode. Without predictions this cannot replace Flo or Clue.

9. Substance

Must replace: Sunnyside, Reframe, I Am Sober, Smoke Free, DrinkControl.

Have today: generic alcohol/tobacco/other logging; amount/unit, date/time, notes; 7-day strip and history.

Missing: no moderation vs sobriety goal tracks; no standard-drink conversion; no money-saved, calories-avoided, or milestone tracking; no cravings/trigger logging; no reduction plans or coaching; no community model; no reminders or streak system.

Add: goal modes such as reduce, quit, and sober-curious; standard drink/cigarette conversion; money, calories, and time-saved dashboard; milestones, streaks, and relapse-aware progress; craving/trigger logging; weekly plan and reminders; motivational content or coaching; exportable progress report.

10. DisciplineEngine

Must replace: Streaks, Habitica, Habit Pixel, Awesome Habits.

Have today: active-day union across logs; streak and medication-adherence percentage; 7-day strip and binary activity graph.

Missing: no user-defined habits; no custom frequency or goals; no reminders, widgets, or nudges; no negative-habit tracking; no habit notes; no heatmaps or deep analytics; no social/gamification layer.

Add: custom habit builder; daily/weekly/monthly schedules and targets; reminders, widgets, and lock-screen actions; good-habit and bad-habit modes; streak rules and heatmaps; habit notes and mood linkage; export.

11. Medications

Must replace: Medisafe, Apple Medications, Mango-style reminders.

Have today: the strongest engine: regimen CRUD, schedule-today, take flow, pause/resume/stop; adherence, refill, duplicate/polypharmacy flags; prescription scan and verify flow; OS reminders with snooze and full-screen call UI.

Missing vs Medisafe: interaction checking is flags-only, not a full checker UX; no refill ordering or pharmacy handoff; no caregiver missed-dose alerts; no appointment manager; no broad measurement-tracker suite; limited complex dosing such as taper, PRN, or cycle-based schedules; no travel/timezone handling; no adherence prediction engine; doctor reporting is not medication-centric enough.

Add: full interaction checker with severity, sources, and doctor-discussion export; refill management with low-supply forecasting; caregiver alerts for missed critical doses; PRN, taper, alternating-day, and complex schedules; timezone travel handling; appointment linkage; medication-centric doctor report; adherence insights; pharmacy handoff where legally appropriate.

12. Voice / VoiceCheckin

Must replace: Echo, Murmur, Speakwise, Day One voice journaling, Dayora-style AI journaling.

Have today: scheduled daily voice call flow; TTS questions for mood, energy, stress, sleep, water; short turns with backend transcription; structured fields saved to DailyLog.

Missing: no free-form journaling; no transcript library or search; no AI summaries or pattern detection; no emotion analysis; no photo/location enrichment; no multilingual support; no playback/export; backend has no streaming, diarization, speaker ID, or session API.

Add: free-form voice journal mode alongside guided check-in; transcript history, search, and favorites; AI summaries, keywords, and emotion tags; weekly voice reflection; longer recordings with pause/resume; multilingual transcription; transcript export; backend session/streaming architecture.

13. Documents / OCR / medical extraction

Must replace: Apple Health Records/FHIR, MyQuest-style lab apps, Eider, clinical-document OCR pipelines.

Have today: PDF/image upload; 4-type classification covering lab, prescription, discharge, imaging; structured extraction and timeline linkage; prescription-med verification; provenance with source references.

Missing: only 4 document types; no OCR correction UI; no folders, tags, or full-text search; no lab-value trend views; no FHIR import/export; no provider/EHR connectivity; no reprocess endpoint; tight bulk-upload limits.

Add: OCR correction and human-review workflow; more document types such as vaccination, allergy, pathology, cardiology, and visit summaries; lab trends with reference ranges in careful display-only wording; document search, tags, folders, favorites; FHIR R4 import/export; provider-sharing pathway; failed-extraction reprocessing; bulk import; per-document export bundle.

14. Report / doctor sharing

Must replace: Apple Share with Provider, Clue Doctor Report, Eider share cards, HealthLog PDF/FHIR export.

Have today: 30-day doctor PDF; OS share sheet; device-side assembly.

Missing: no selective data/time-range builder; no per-engine reports; no FHIR/IPS export; no clinician cover summary; no secure time-boxed sharing; no sharing audit trail; no scheduled reports.

Add: share builder with engines, date range, and detail level; clinician-friendly cover page with medications, allergies, conditions, and recent changes; FHIR R4 / International Patient Summary export; per-engine PDFs; secure expiring share link if the backend supports it; sharing history and access log; CSV/JSON companion export.

15. Trends / Timeline / Insights / Chat

Must replace: Bearable correlations, Apple Health trends, Google Health insights, Ada-style guidance, with the boundary below.

Have today: cross-engine averages and correlations; unified timeline and diary views; curated dashboard feed; grounded longitudinal chat with citations; deterministic summaries and provenance.

Missing: no chat streaming; no voice input in production chat; no conversation export; no custom date-range comparison; no CSV export; insights lack direct actions and reminders; no goal-tracking linkage; no multilingual chat; relatively basic correlation math.

Add: streaming chat responses; voice input/output in chat; conversation and insight-report export; custom date comparisons; CSV export; one next action or reminder per insight; goal tracking linked to trends; stronger anomaly explanations; multilingual support. Do not turn chat into a ranked-diagnosis symptom checker without regulated clinical governance.

16. VitalsLogger / DailyLog and platform sync

Must replace: Apple Health manual logging, Google Health, Bearable all-in-one tracking.

Have today: excellent central manual capture across mood, sleep, symptoms, pain/body map, activity, meds, vitals, nutrition, and notes; offline-first SQLite with idempotent sync.

Missing: no HealthKit sync; no Health Connect sync; no wearable auto-import; no smart-scale/BP/glucose device import; no CSV import; no duplicate-merge workflow; no quick-log widgets, shortcuts, or lock-screen actions.

Add: bidirectional HealthKit sync where appropriate; Health Connect sync on Android; wearable auto-import for sleep, HR, HRV, activity, and weight; smart-scale and BP/glucose pathways; CSV import; duplicate detection and merge; widgets, shortcuts, and quick-log actions; background sync reliability dashboard. Treat this as P0 platform work.

Priority build order

*TIP
Platform sync unlocks almost every other engine. Manual logging alone cannot beat passive competitors.

P0 — platform parity: HealthKit plus Health Connect sync; wearable import for HR/HRV/sleep/activity/weight; reminders beyond medications; export framework covering CSV, JSON, PDF, and FHIR.

P1 — engine depth: sleep timestamps, consistency, stages, smart alarm; nutrition micros, recipes, portion correction, voice logging; structured fitness logging and plans; cycle predictions or explicit non-predictive positioning; substance goals, standard units, milestones; custom habits and widgets.

P2 — clinical and sharing maturity: medication interaction depth, caregiver alerts, refill forecasting; document correction UI, lab trends, FHIR export; selective doctor-share builder and IPS bundle; chat streaming/export and actionable insights.

P3 — differentiation: recovery strain targets and habit correlation; voice journaling library and AI reflections; advanced readiness baselines; German/EU provider-sharing workflows.
